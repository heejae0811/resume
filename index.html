Research

Climbing and hiking with the Yonsei University Alpine Club sparked my interest in what makes a climb work. That curiosity became the starting point for my graduate research into climbing performance analysis.

Human Movement Analysis Computer Vision Machine Learning Explainable AI LLMs

Thesis Research

My thesis analyzes climbing videos using pose estimation, classifies movement patterns with machine learning, then feeds explainable-AI (XAI) output into an LLM to generate coaching feedback in plain, accessible language.

Featured Project

An AI-based mobile and web application built from my thesis research. It analyzes uploaded climbing videos and returns personalized, plain-language coaching feedback.
